## Publishing cadence recommendation

## Short answer

For a new or small site, publishing `2-3 strong posts per week` is usually better operationally than dropping `15-20 posts` in one batch.

## Why

- Search engines do not require daily posting to rank.
- Bulk publishing is not a penalty by itself, but it is harder to maintain quality, internal links, and post-launch updates.
- Smaller weekly batches make it easier to:
  - improve interlinking
  - submit for indexing
  - watch what gets impressions
  - refresh titles and intros if needed

## Best practical model for Shot AI

1. Publish 3-4 cornerstone pages first
2. Then move to 2-3 posts per week
3. Refresh and relink older posts every week

## Scheduling note

This static site does not have a native CMS scheduler today. To auto-schedule publication, you would need:

- a GitHub Action that publishes queued drafts on dates, or
- an external CMS / automation layer that pushes to the repo on schedule
