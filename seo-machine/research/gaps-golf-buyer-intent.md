# /research-gaps

Date: 2026-04-10

## Golf buyer-intent content gaps

### What competitors overdo

- Long affiliate roundups with weak explanation
- Expensive gadget recommendations too early
- One-size-fits-all promises for multiple swing faults

### What they underdo

- Explaining which aid matches which swing issue
- Telling players what to buy first versus later
- Connecting equipment choice to actual feedback loops

## Gap for Shot AI

- Build "buy smarter" golf content with honest sequencing:
  - simple aids first
  - diagnosis before purchase
  - video review before expensive gadgets
