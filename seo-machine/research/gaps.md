# /research-gaps

Date: 2026-04-10

## Competitor content gaps

### What competitors do well

- Strong category naming around "AI golf coach" and "golf swing analyzer"
- Clear benefit statements around instant feedback
- Feature-heavy App Store descriptions

### What competitors under-explain

- How analysis helps during real practice sessions
- Cross-sport positioning outside a single niche
- Educational content that captures search traffic before the app install
- Trust assets on the web outside the App Store

## Gaps Shot AI can exploit

- Publish plain-English articles that explain how AI coaching works
- Build separate golf and tennis landing pages
- Add FAQ content that answers pre-download objections
- Create internal links between homepage, comparison pages, and sport-specific articles

## Messaging gap

Many competitors sound like tool catalogs. Shot AI can sound more like a coaching workflow:

- Record
- Review
- Adjust
- Repeat
