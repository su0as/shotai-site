5 Golf Practice Habits That Actually Help You Improve

Good golf practice is not about emptying a bucket. It is about creating repetition with purpose, checking whether the change is real, and leaving the range with something more useful than a temporary feel.

Shot AIReview the movement behind the rep instead of judging the session by one good shot.
  1. Pick one swing problem per session.
  2. Alternate block practice with target practice.
  3. Film a small sample of swings.
  4. Track strike and start line, not just distance.
  5. Finish by writing the next focus.

Why these habits work

They force you to connect practice to feedback. That keeps the session from turning into random ball-hitting and makes the next practice block easier to build on.
